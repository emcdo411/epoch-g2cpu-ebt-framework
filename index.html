02 The CVD Construct — Framework Contribution
NEW EBT CONSTRUCT BIESMANS + McDONALD (2026)

Circular Validation Dependency (CVD)

CVD describes the condition where an AI system is employed to validate a safety-critical architecture it is structurally unqualified to assess because the validation tool and the system under test share the same probabilistic foundation. The result is a closed loop where evaluative bias is never surfaced because the evaluator and the evaluated cannot be meaningfully separated. CVD is Objectivity Laundering operating at its most dangerous scale.

STEP 01

AI is introduced to assist rocket test validation

STEP 02 · BREAK

AI output is treated as deterministic test verdict

STEP 03 · FAILURE

Hallucination surface is invisible until system-level failure

STEP 04

Trust boundary was never defined, so CVD was never detected

03 NNN Governing Layer — Why Natan's Instinct Is Already NNN

NNN-D · DETECTION MODE

Detecting Output Drift Before It Becomes Structural

NNN-D monitors AI output for deviation from defined engineering criteria. In Natan's context, any AI-generated test result that cannot be traced to a deterministic substrate triggers a detection flag before it enters an engineering decision chain.

NNN-G · GOVERNANCE MODE

Enforcing the Trust Boundary Between AI and Execution

NNN-G defines where AI authority ends and engineer authority begins. In rocket architecture, AI governs pattern recognition and anomaly flagging. G²CPU governs deterministic execution.

NNN-H · HUMAN OVERRIDE MODE

Preserving Engineer Judgment as the Final Authority

NNN-H ensures that no AI output, regardless of confidence score, can override a qualified engineer's judgment on a safety-critical decision. This makes Natan's instinct documented and auditable.
